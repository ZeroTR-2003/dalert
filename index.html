(02) — HOW IT WORKS
1

The guard taps once

The app captures a selfie, a GPS point with its accuracy, and a timestamp the device cannot fake, then checks the point against the site's geofence.

verified or flagged, instantly
2

It saves before it sends

Remote posts have bad signal, so every event saves to the phone first with its original capture time, then syncs untouched when the network returns.

nothing editable after the fact
3

A supervisor signs off

The dashboard shows each event with its photo and map pin. Clean shifts are approved in bulk; flags are cleared with a note that stays on the record.

about four minutes a day
